Strive is a self-evolving agent harness built in two phases: a working vertical slice of the gated loop — execute → observe → evaluate → diagnose → propose → validate → accept/reject → retain — over executable strategy code in isolated subprocesses, followed by a deep research pass over six external systems (Flex/GEPA, [prime-agent](https://github.com/primeintellect-ai/prime-agent), the Continual Harness paper arXiv:2605.09998, [exoharness/exo](https://github.com/exoharness/exo), Recursive Language Models, NVIDIA's NOOA) with repos pinned at exact commit SHAs and code-level inspection. The central finding: every researched system built half of strive — GEPA validates candidates rigorously but keeps no durable lineage, while prime-agent, Continual Harness, and exo persist and self-modify richly but validate nothing, with documented drift and stall failures as the consequence. That evidence drove 13 architectural decisions, a trusted-kernel platform design supporting both offline and bounded online evolution, and a 7-stage roadmap whose optional final stage co-evolves harness behavior and model weights. The prototype passes 23 offline tests with strict typing and ships a committed demo ledger showing acceptance, restart persistence, rollback, and branching lineage.

- Validated slice: seed strategy fails negative-integer cases; weakness diagnosed from trace evidence alone, patched, validated in a fresh sandbox, accepted 0.571 → 1.000 with zero regressions, journaled with full lineage.
- Key research evidence: the Continual Harness paper documents an inherited-usage collapse to 6.4% and an 842-repetition stall — exactly the failures strive's acceptance gate, inheritance-aware thresholds, and trusted stall detection are designed to prevent.
- Architecture invariants: trust boundaries by mechanism not policy, all metrics computed trusted-side, failure-as-score, append-only history, model I/O journaled for offline replay.
