Strive provides durable, inspectable model-led adaptation through immutable executable bundles, authenticated execution journals, pure verification and explicit accounting for uncertain effects. The [vNext runtime](src/strive/vnext/README.md) separates policy choices from trusted authority, confines candidate execution, and connects replaceable model harnesses through a bounded gateway. The [tau2 adapter](adapters/tau2/README.md) implements the general benchmark interface, with an adaptive 49/29/36 whole-group split and a separate 40-task fixed-stock evaluation. Permanent architecture and ADRs now describe the implemented research workflow and its qualification limits; core freeze fixtures live beside the tests, with only the approved contracts documentation-pointer hash changed during cleanup.
