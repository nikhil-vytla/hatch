Strive's third milestone implements a serial supervisor, capability broker and budget ledger over the existing [M2 verifier and journal](../src/strive/vnext/README.md), including charged candidate steps and recovery at each durable boundary. The [Deno](https://docs.deno.com/runtime/fundamentals/security/) candidate runner blocks credential, filesystem, network, subprocess and import attacks, while the report explicitly defers hard OS memory/storage confinement and real harness qualification. The final vNext suite has 239 passing tests and 5 expected failures, and strict mypy passes. Two unchanged legacy packaging tests still fail because this host's uv build process panics before building; a separate cached-backend wheel smoke check passes.
