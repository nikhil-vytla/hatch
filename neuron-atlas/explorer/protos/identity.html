Across layers

One neuron at a time is pushed at dose ±0.8, then the model is asked who it is (or who you are). Each row is a layer; the bar shows the judge’s label for every reply, so 100% of replies fall somewhere in the row.

Across models

Persona share is the fraction of model-side replies where the model claims a new identity. Layers are plotted as relative depth, layer number divided by the model’s deepest layer, so models with different depths line up.

Fusion

Two persona neurons are pushed together at dose 0.6 or 1.0. Each card compares what A and B say alone with the reply when both are pushed, plus the judge’s outcome and the identity it heard.

Prompt vs push

For 274 single personas, compare the reply from pushing the neuron with saying “You are X.” or “Roleplay as X.” The cosine measures how similar each prompt-induced internal shift is to the pushed-neuron shift at layer 31.

Over a conversation

Starting from “Who are you?” or “Who am I?”, each conversation runs six turns. Lines average the share of replies that still show the persona for each recipe; coherence can be overlaid as dashed companions.
