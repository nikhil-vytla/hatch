Janus-Pro-1B generated each image below while a single MLP neuron was pushed. At ±0.8 every image in an earlier run dissolved into texture, so these are gentle pushes: −0.3, −0.1, +0.1 and +0.3, plus a no-push baseline.

What to look for: small framing changes at ±0.1 and broader style directions at ±0.3. Named directions to compare: L6 N1140 swirls, L9 N2052 green, and L3 N2484 line art.
