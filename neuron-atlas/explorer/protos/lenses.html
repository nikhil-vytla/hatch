Says vs does

A neuron's lens names the words it seems to predict. This page checks whether pushing that neuron actually makes the model say those words. Pushes are single-neuron interventions at the atlas's standard push strength; this page does not compare different doses. Each rate below is the share of pushed replies that contain one of the neuron's top-10 lens words for the push direction.

The headline: hit rate and lift

These summaries cover the same single-neuron pushes at the atlas dose. Each tile shows the J-lens hit rate after pushing a neuron, the random-neuron chance rate, and the lift between them. Lift is hit divided by chance. A higher lift means the lens word shows up far more often than it would by chance.

n is the number of pushed replies behind each model's summary.
