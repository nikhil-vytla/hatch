Hearing

What a push changes

A push is one neuron pushed at one dose while the model hears one sound; both doses (-1 and +1) are counted together. “Fixed” counts pushes that turn a wrong answer right; “broke” counts pushes that turn a right answer wrong.

The sounds

Twenty-two short clips: 6 synthetic tones and 16 ESC-50 recordings. Each card shows the true class and what the unpushed model answered. Click a card to select that sound; the selected card gets a violet outline. Play buttons stream one clip at a time.

Pushed answers for the selected sound

Every neuron and dose for the selected sound, with blocks B0–B23 and doses -1 or +1. A row whose answer differs from the unpushed answer carries an eosin-tinted “changed” badge. Rows are sorted by block.

Neurons that force one sound

The top 12 pushes by how many of the 22 sounds they take over. Each was pushed at -1 or +1 in one audio block (B0–B23); the selected card is outlined in violet. Open a card to see that push’s answer for every sound, with a play button beside each one.

Change by audio block

Bars show the share of pushed answers that changed, block by block; both doses (-1 and +1) are pooled. Early blocks and the last block change it most; the middle blocks change it least. Eosin bars; hover or focus a bar for its counts.
