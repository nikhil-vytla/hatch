Story casting

Both prompts stop right where the story introduces someone, so the first words of the continuation are the character. Every neuron was pushed one at a time at −0.5 and +0.5 (in units of the layer's median residual size), and changing one neuron can change who walks in. Characters are read with a rule-based parse, not a judge.
