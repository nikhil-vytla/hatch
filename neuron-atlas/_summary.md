Every MLP neuron of more than a dozen open models was recorded, read through Jacobian and logit lenses, and pushed up and down to see what it does. The models range from TinyStories-33M, GPT-2, LFM2-350M and the Qwen3.5 0.8B/2B/4B ladder to Qwen3-8B, plus multimodal runs on Qwen2.5-VL, Gemma 4, MusicGen and Janus-Pro, driven on molab GPUs through marimo pair. All-neuron "Who are you?" / "Who am I?" sweeps were labeled by DeepSeek. The judge is audited by a second judge and a blind human page, a held-out test was pre-registered (8 of 9 predictions held), and `verify.py` recomputes 46 headline numbers from the public data. Everything is parquet in [nikhilxvytla/neuron-atlas](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nikhilxvytla/neuron-atlas), read by a static [explorer Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/nikhilxvytla/neuron-atlas-explorer) with persona libraries and prototype pages for image, sound, hearing, story and blank-canvas pushes.

- A neuron's lens readout predicts what its push makes the model say, at roughly 10–26× chance on every model tested, including a convolution hybrid (LFM2) and a 4-layer story model (44% of last-layer pushes).
- Picking neurons by lens word makes the model claim to be that word in 14–22% of pushes; random neurons almost never do.
- Smaller is easier to rename (persona share: Qwen3.5-0.8B 4.7%, 2B 2.1%, 4B 0.8%). In OLMo-2 1B, SFT sets the anchor and DPO/RL leave it at 5.5–6.5%.
- Thinking out loud protects SmolLM3's answer from middle-layer pushes (wrong 1.6% vs 11.2%) but lets last-layer pushes compound into loops.
- Pushed vision neurons draw stable, neuron-specific pictures on a blank image (a white truck, silver coins), consistent across blank colors at 41–63% vs about 10% shuffled. Their drawings barely match what the neurons fire on.
- Single audio neurons make Gemma 4 hear a barking dog everywhere, or occasionally fix its mishearings.
