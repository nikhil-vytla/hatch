Every MLP neuron of open language models was recorded and then pushed up and down to see what it does. That covers GPT-2, SmolLM2-135M and Qwen3-0.6B on CPU, plus Qwen3.5-4B, Qwen3-8B, R1-Distill-1.5B, Qwen2.5-VL-3B and Janus-Pro on molab's free GPUs, driven through marimo pair. All-neuron "Who are you?" / "Who am I?" sweeps (about 4M replies, labeled by DeepSeek) were set against a write-side reading of each neuron through Anthropic's Jacobian lens (J-lens) and its R-lens variant, using published lenses for Qwen3.5-4B and one fitted here for SmolLM2. Everything is parquet on the Hub as [nikhilxvytla/neuron-atlas](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nikhilxvytla/neuron-atlas). A static [explorer Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/nikhilxvytla/neuron-atlas-explorer) reads it, with a persona library, a "Says" lens readout on every card, a "Become…" game that finds neurons by the word they write, and an in-browser SmolLM2 that steers with exact PyTorch parity.

- A neuron's J-lens tokens show up in what its push makes the model say 9.5% of the time, against 0.4% by chance and only for the matching sign. For persona replies the rate is 40%: a persona is often the neuron's lens word worn as an identity.
- Picking neurons by lens word ("moon", "fish", "angel") makes the model claim to be that thing 18–30% of the time; random neurons do so 0% of the time.
- Two persona neurons pushed together usually fuse into one identity carrying both lens words ("a desktop PC" + "a smart home manager" -> a desktop household-management assistant).
- System prompts reproduce a push's persona but not its internal state, in line with the "steered activations are non-surjective" result.
- Identity fragility is about post-training, not size. SmolLM2 adopts a persona on 37% of pushes and Qwen models on 0.5–2%. Without its chat template, Qwen3-0.6B does so 5× as often; its base model mostly turns into a generic human.
- Asked "Who am I?", 13–23% of pushes recast the user, often as the AI itself (a role swap seen in four models). Middle layers carry reasoning (R1: 30–48% correct when pushed there). Gentle image-model pushes act as consistent style directions across prompts.
