Every MLP neuron of GPT-2, SmolLM2-135M-Instruct and Qwen3-0.6B (169k neurons in all) was recorded on a shared 2,500-document corpus. For each neuron the atlas keeps its top documents with per-token activations, the logit lens of its output direction, and replies with the neuron pushed up or down, in the style of [neuron-explorer](https://github.com/lyramakesmusic/neuron-explorer) and the Qwen3-8B persona library. DeepSeek-flash labeled about 3,500 neurons and sorted every steered reply, and each label was checked on held-out documents against a shuffled-label null. The parquet output feeds a static explorer (hyparquet plus a DuckDB-WASM SQL tab, with a map, stained examples, pushes against random controls, a cross-model search and a guessing game), a Gradio live app, and a [marimo](https://molab.marimo.io) notebook that runs larger models on molab's free RTX Pro 6000 GPUs and publishes to a Hugging Face dataset.

- Labels pass the held-out check at 0.75–0.82 against a 0.50 null, and the labeler's confidence predicts quality: in a 50-label hand audit, confidence-5 labels were good 9 of 10 times and confidence ≤3 labels 2 of 15.
- In these small models, whether a push makes a persona or breaks the text does not depend on which neuron is pushed: random neurons do it as often as the most peaked ones.
- What the pushed reply talks about does depend on the neuron: +0.5 replies contain the neuron's own logit-lens words 6.5–20% of the time, against 1–5.5% for another neuron's words.
- Steering doses that are safe for 4B models (±1 × the residual norm) break a 135M model; ±0.5 stays coherent.
