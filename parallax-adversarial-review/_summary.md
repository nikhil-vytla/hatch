Parallax’s variant layer is a typed contract catalog with structural admission checks, not yet a reproducible autoresearch loop: `TaskSpec`/`TaskVariant` never join the executable `Recipe`→HUD→`grade_candidate` path, Evolving Intent trajectories compile but do not inject multi-turn messages or enforce staged writes, and budgets/verifier policies remain unused labels. Live calibration already separated platform failures by hand, but `calibration.py` still lacks a harness-vs-model outcome classifier and uses fragile mean-rate thresholds on tiny N. The smallest credible prototype is one admitted baseline plus one hand-authored staged EI twin under a frozen, budget-matched harness, scored with the shared sealed verifier, recorded as classified RunRecords, and compared with paired statistics—deferring the other eight families and non-REUSE verifier policies until that effect reproduces.
