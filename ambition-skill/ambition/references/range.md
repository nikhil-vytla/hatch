# Range

Range means looking at a task through more fields than one person could practically consult. Subagents and multi-agent setups make this cheap: each field can run in parallel with its own context.

Range also works on the human. Seeing someone in a different field use a tool in an unexpected way is often what prompts the jump. In the source article, a colleague used Blender for interior-design renders, and that pushed the author to consider 3D scenes for a personal homepage.

## Nouns → verbs

Nouns hide which fields matter, and verbs expose them. (The technique comes from Tom Kelley and Jonathan Littman's *The Art of Innovation*.)

| Noun | Verbs | Fields that are expert at the verbs |
|---|---|---|
| A quarterly business review deck | Get busy people to believe a cause-and-effect story; let them decide under uncertainty in 30 minutes | Data journalism, editing, decision analysis, motion and audio design, trial advocacy |
| An onboarding flow | Teach a stranger a new skill; build trust before asking for anything | Instructional design, game tutorial design, hospitality, sales |
| A bug report | Let someone else reproduce a failure cheaply; rank it against other work | QA, forensics, incident command, triage medicine |

## Assembling the crew

Pick 3–6 disciplines from the verbs. For each, write:
- **Method**: the concrete activity it performs, such as running a regression, cutting the storyline to three beats, or scoring the audio.
- **Artefact**: what it hands back.
- **Adversary**: at least one crew member exists to break the conclusion. A short seller looks for the reason the story is false. A forensic accountant checks whether the numbers tie out. A red team attacks the system.

## Activities, not personas

"You are a grizzled short seller…" mostly makes transcripts theatrical and seldom improves the work. Assign what that person *does*: "Research the strongest case that this growth story is wrong. Find the metric most likely to be flattering the narrative, and quantify how much it moves the conclusion." Name the role only as a label for a bundle of methods.

## Completion check

- Every noun in the task has been restated as at least one verb.
- Every crew member has one named method and one artefact.
- At least one crew member is adversarial.
- The crew's outputs feed a single synthesis step, so they don't stay as parallel essays.
