Felix Rieseberg's essay [How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models](https://felixrieseberg.com/how-to-be-more-ambitious-a-tutorial-for-humans-with-models/) is distilled here into `ambition`, an Agent Skill in the [agentskills.io](https://agentskills.io/specification) format. The skill turns a plain request into an "ambition ladder" along three axes. Altitude aims at the outcome rather than the task. Depth means working with a top professional's instruments, raw data and an outside judge. Range means restating nouns as verbs and assembling a crew of disciplines that includes adversaries. The skill body is a six-step procedure with completion criteria. Per-axis techniques, a worked Acme Inc. example, self-efficacy evidence and attribution are disclosed references, and a ready-to-paste "deeper, not bigger" prompt is an asset.

- Passes the reference validator (`uvx --from skills-ref agentskills validate`). Note that the PyPI executable is `agentskills`, not `skills-ref`.
- Every rung must name an outside verifier, access needs and a cost to the user. This turns the essay's warning against self-grading into a hard requirement.
- Not yet evaluated for behaviour or trigger accuracy.
