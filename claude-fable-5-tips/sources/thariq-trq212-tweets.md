## Summary (paraphrased, not verbatim)

Across this thread, Thariq frames Fable 5 as enough of a capability jump
that people should raise their ambitions for what they hand it, rather
than testing it on the same small tasks they gave prior models. One
specific, recurring piece of advice attributed to him — and echoed
directly in Simon Willison's post — is to have Fable decide for itself
which weaker/cheaper model is appropriate for a given coding subtask and
run that subtask in a subagent, reserving Fable's own turn for judgement
calls rather than mechanical implementation. A separate post in the
thread describes using Fable to edit its own launch video, offered as a
demonstration of end-to-end delegation rather than a prompting tip per
se.
