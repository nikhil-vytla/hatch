Felix Rieseberg's essay [How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models](https://felixrieseberg.com/how-to-be-more-ambitious-a-tutorial-for-humans-with-models/) is distilled here into `be-ambitious`, an Agent Skill in the [agentskills.io](https://agentskills.io/specification) format. The skill turns a plain request into an "ambition ladder" along three axes: altitude (aim at the outcome), depth (a top practitioner's instruments, raw data and an outside judge) and range (nouns restated as verbs, plus a crew of disciplines that includes adversaries). Each rung must name its verifier, access needs and cost. Evals on the free OpenCode-hosted DeepSeek V4.1 Flash compared the skill against a plain and a length-matched baseline across 10 requests. They found that the skill clearly shifts what the model proposes. They also exposed two failure modes, which drove a v2 revision that is not yet evaluated.

- Blind pairwise judge: the skill won 87% of comparisons against the baseline and 79% against a length-matched baseline. Trigger precision was 1.00 and recall 0.97.
- Example anchoring: with references loaded, DuckDB from the worked example appeared in 11 of 30 answers (0 in every other condition), including launch emails.
- Overreach: the skill lost when rungs needed access the user lacked, or asked the model for numbers it couldn't compute.
- The judge is the same model as the generator, and the rubric is near-circular. The free endpoint went down before v2 could be re-run.
