# Range

Look at the work through more fields than one person could practically consult. Subagents make this cheap.

**Nouns → verbs** (from Kelley and Littman, *The Art of Innovation*). A quarterly review isn't "a deck". It's a way to get busy people to believe a cause-and-effect story and to decide under uncertainty in 30 minutes. The verbs show which fields are good at the job.

**Assemble the crew.** For that review you might bring in an analyst, a data journalist, an editor, and a motion and audio designer. Add a red team too: a short seller and a forensic accountant, each trying to break the story.

**Activities, not personas.** Have the model do what a short seller does to research a position, rather than play one. Role play makes transcripts strange without improving the work.

**Look sideways.** Seeing a tool used outside its home field is often what reveals the leap. Blender used for interior-design renders is one example.
