Parallax defines a research model for controlled perturbations of agent tasks and environments, with explicit public inputs, sealed evaluator authority, admission invariants, matched arms, and estimands. Its content-addressed core now implements GSM8K source and asset provenance, public and sealed task identity, native grading, atomic publication, and locked replay. [Evolving Intent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20734v1) remains a documented synthesis strategy, with guidance tied to an immutable [Microsoft reference revision](https://github.com/microsoft/evolving-intent/tree/993d6be9597ac03854b46362ccd647eb1bfd267a) but no runtime dependency. Executable synthesis and experiment execution are not present, and no generated-data, byte-level, or paper-score reproduction is claimed.
