Parallax implements and tests one offline [Evolving Intent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20734v1) path from real-shaped GSM8K rows through shared extracted intents, strict frozen Pydantic models, native grading, deterministic JSONL evidence, and source-clustered reporting. Discriminated event, outcome, and evidence unions make each retained variant explicit, while branded source identities, answers, digests, seeds, and trial indices keep semantic primitives distinct. A preregistered manifest distinguishes absent rows from recorded failures, and identification bounds with a closed-form Hoeffding interval keep failed runs outside model pass-rate denominators. The slice follows the semantics of the immutable [Microsoft reference revision](https://github.com/microsoft/evolving-intent/tree/993d6be9597ac03854b46362ccd647eb1bfd267a) without claiming a real-provider run, upstream seed reproducibility, provider-text parity, generated-pool reproduction, or paper-score reproduction.
