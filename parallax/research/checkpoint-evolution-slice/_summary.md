Parallax's second synthesis strategy, checkpoint evolution, now runs end to end offline: an agent extends its own persisting workspace across an ordered checkpoint sequence while sealed test obligations accumulate, and checkpoint N is gated on checkpoints 1..N-1 still passing. The slice follows the mechanics of [SlopCodeBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24755) (per-stage strict/isolated/core verdicts, automatic regression reclassification, black-box entrypoint-only grading, continue-on-failure with censoring of missing workspaces) and the repo conventions of the [Evolving Intent slice](https://github.com/nikhil-vytla/hatch/pull/11): strict frozen Pydantic models, digest-chained evidence receipts, and validators that make ungated or partially delivered episodes unrepresentable. A hand-verified three-checkpoint CLI seed family passes five executable admission gates and exercises both the `evolved` arm and the `carry-reference` control with scripted agents; no paid inference ran.

- Admission is bidirectional and recorded: incremental gold references must pass every accumulated suite, and every checkpoint must fail on the prior stage's reference (no vacuous stages).
- Deferred and recorded: quality-measurement panel, the three remaining controlled arms, agent-assisted family synthesis with its compute-priced gates, and sandboxing for real-agent code execution.
