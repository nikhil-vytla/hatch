Parallax's second synthesis strategy, checkpoint evolution, went from design to executed paid screening in this unit: an agent extends its own persisting workspace across an ordered checkpoint sequence while sealed test obligations accumulate, following the mechanics of [SlopCodeBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24755) with strict frozen Pydantic models, digest-chained receipts, container-sandboxed verification, and validators that make ungated episodes unrepresentable. The preregistered 60-call Haiku screening ran unchanged for $0.28 (against a $5 cap) and produced a complete stage-3 separation: the carry-reference arm verified strict on 10/10 seeds while the evolved arm, re-serializing its own increasingly verbose workspace, blew the family's 4096-byte budget on 10/10 seeds — the self-accumulation signature surfacing as budget pressure rather than semantic regression. Evidence completeness was 100% with zero infrastructure failures, so the preregistered decision rule says proceed to multi-family synthesis ([PR #27](https://github.com/nikhil-vytla/hatch/pull/27)).
