Cafe Jev turns a drink lookup into a fictional cafe where customers clarify preferences, customize legal recipes and receive a simulated receipt. A shared catalog defines 304 recipes, exact prices and seeded stock, while [Jev](https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction) interprets language and code rejects unsupported choices. The published fixture covers all 81 core partial preference states plus 21 authored cases involving revisions, negation, ambiguity and impossible requests; raw model mistakes and provider failures remain visible. [Motion](https://motion.dev/docs/react) animates the customer and cup, and private customer goals independently score completed orders without entering model requests.
