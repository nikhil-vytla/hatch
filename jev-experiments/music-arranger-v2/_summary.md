Music arranger v2 uses one eight-bar score for six synthesized tracks, the piano roll, note editing and MIDI export, with contextual Jev phrase selection, bar-aligned changes, locks and cancellation guards. Fourteen recorded arrangements cover all eight original briefs plus six explicit contour briefs, preserving 70 successful calls and their inputs, candidates and provider history. Selection remains repetitive: 31 of 32 phrases on the original briefs are arches, explicit contour requests match in 17 of 24 phrases, and eight matched order/identifier probes all choose arch in one fixed context. The implementation uses [Tone.js](https://tonejs.github.io/) and [Tone MIDI](https://github.com/Tonejs/Midi); automated parity and browser checks pass, but no listening study supports a subjective quality claim.
