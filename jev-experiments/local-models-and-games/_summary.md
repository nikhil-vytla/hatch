A native MLX port and specialist fine-tune of Laya, three open language models, a full-option scoring variant, and a real Core ML export were compared with Jev on all 400 test cases from [Typed Decisions](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions). Jev reached 72.75% synthetic-teacher agreement; Qwen3 4B reached 54.05%, while the trained Laya head improved to 46.95% but failed to beat a 48.95% label-prior baseline. The Core ML export had similar aggregate quality but changed 60 of 2,000 decisions, demonstrating why complete-case checks matter. The [Jev experiments app](https://jev-experiments.vercel.app) adds animated Snake and 3D rescue games, full-text model comparisons, clearer categories, and a research map with eight benchmark candidates and twelve proposed experiences.
