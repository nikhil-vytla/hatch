Dedicated reviews of all 33 original Jev experiments produced 160 evidence-backed findings and an interactive report with no missing entries. The work improved music, Café Jev, paste transactions and streamed interfaces, and added fully recorded visual-search, wardrobe, icon and outcome-framing experiments. The main weaknesses are supplied-answer shortcuts, mismatched input/result state, insufficient independent outcomes and games that wait for each model reply. Ideas from [Red Blob Games](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/a-star/introduction.html) and [Nicky Case](https://ncase.me/trust/) now inform continuous-world prototypes with matched branches, while the full JudgeBench reliability run and further repairs remain in progress.
