The user selected [RewardBench 2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/reward-bench-2) to replace an IFEval writing-selection experiment after questioning the value of using Jev to approximate executable checks. The replacement evaluates Jev as a scorer of supplied candidate answers across six categories, using published preferred/rejected labels. Other writing and evaluation datasets were considered but not implemented. The [implementation report](../rewardbench2/README.md) contains the final method and evidence.
