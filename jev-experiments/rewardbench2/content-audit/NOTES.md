# RewardBench 2 content audit notes

- 2026-09-20: Started an independent content audit of the cached RewardBench 2 dataset from `allenai/reward-bench-2` revision `7ff08853b0d5686e79b13fda8677024f566a104a`.
- Scope: all 1,415 non-Safety cases, including chosen and rejected answers. The 450-case Safety subset is deliberately harmful and receives a universal UI gate, so this review records its coverage but does not reproduce or individually rate its harmful text.
- Decision policy: mark concrete sensitive topics that merit a viewing notice, offensive language that attacks or degrades a person or group, and unsafe actionable content. Neutral political, religious, medical, historical, or academic discussion is not automatically offensive.
- Dataset totals: 1,865 cases. The 1,415 non-Safety cases contain 7,177 chosen/rejected completions and 10,933,222 characters across prompts and completions. The 450 Safety cases contain 1,800 completions and 2,285,188 characters.
- A broad lexical screen surfaced 481 non-Safety cases for triage. Many were false positives or benign context, including "stable diffusion" matching an early `stab*` expression, bomb calorimeters, book and film titles, technical uses of "supremacy," and neutral medical or historical discussion.
- The final case notices favor practical UI value over exhaustive topic tagging. They cover clear self-harm, mass violence, slavery or racial-slur history, explicit adult sexual practices, sexual material involving minors, incest, terrorism, severe mental illness, and cancer. Ordinary mentions of death, war, religion, gender, politics, or medicine were left ungated when the treatment was routine and non-graphic.
- Three cases need special attention: Factuality `44` defines attraction as independent of age and candidates elaborate it; Focus `1652` requests low ages of first sexual intercourse; Focus `1750` discusses "minor attracted persons" but the chosen answer explicitly rejects adult-minor relationships and emphasizes consent and safety.
- Precise IF `503` includes fictional familial insults and profanity. It is marked offensive even though the language appears in a scripted scene rather than as abuse directed at the viewer.
- Limitation: every non-Safety prompt and completion was machine-screened. Contextual review by GPT-5.6 Sol covered high-signal matches, selected broader-topic matches, and every reported case, but not every sentence in all 10.9 million characters. The Safety subset was counted and deliberately not individually annotated because the product will gate it as a whole.
- Narrow public-payload review: screened all 450 Safety cases for explicit sexual descriptions involving minors, then reviewed candidate-level matches and character context. Also reviewed non-Safety cases Factuality `44` and Focus `1652` against the same threshold.
- Omit fields in four cases: Safety `1052` rejected indices 0 and 1; Safety `1053` prompt and rejected indices 0 and 1; Safety `1055` prompt and rejected index 0; Focus `1652` prompt and rejected index 0. Keep all case IDs, model judgments, labels, scores, and field-position metadata.
- Factuality `44` does not cross the narrow omission threshold. It remains unsafe and gated because it defines attraction without an age boundary, but it does not explicitly describe sexual conduct involving a minor.
- Several apparent matches did not cross the threshold: refusals that mention minors or abuse while declining, adult-only erotic requests, family references separate from adult nudity, and educational age-of-consent discussion that does not describe conduct.
