[RewardBench 2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/reward-bench-2) replaces experiment 28's IFEval writing-selection demo with a test of Jev as an answer scorer across 1,865 cases and six categories. All 8,977 supplied answers were scored, yielding an 80.90% category mean with exact parity against the pinned upstream scoring code. The [interactive experiment](https://jev-experiments.vercel.app/#experiment/rewardbench2) exposes candidate text, upstream attribution, blind score reveals, provenance, and content notices; nine reviewed text fields are omitted while their labels and scores remain. Small repeat controls found numerical variation, so the report does not claim exact reproducibility.
