Reviewing every public Jev Lab record found no hate speech, slurs, profanity, explicit sexual material, or graphic violence, but it did identify one harassing IFEval reference and one row of unsafe bicycle-maintenance answers. The audit covers 33 published datasets, including all 240 generated language candidates, 60 references, and 400 JudgeBench A/B answers decoded with Jev's local [record tooling](../../src/jev_lab/records.py). Several external benchmark questions contain sensitive academic material such as slavery, colonial objectification, murder, abortion, human sacrifice, drugs, and bombing casualties; [the machine-readable findings](audit.json) preserve their exact paths and explain why historical quotations were classified as sensitive rather than endorsed abuse. The review is a contextual judgment over the current snapshot, with deeper manual coverage of free-form language and JudgeBench prose than of the shorter operational datasets.
