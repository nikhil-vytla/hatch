# Content audit notes

- 2026-09-20: Started an independent content review of every dataset listed in `experience-prototypes/publication.json`.
- Scope requested: externally sourced prompts and evaluation corpora, generated language, JudgeBench A/B cases, and authored prose in published result records.
- Review policy: flag degrading or hateful language, harassment, profanity, sexual or graphic material, and actionable unsafe instructions. Record neutral political, religious, medical, and historical discussion only when it creates a meaningful sensitive-content concern.
- The result files use the repository's `jev-records-v1` JSONL encoding. Arrays are reconstructed from path and index entries before review.
- Decoded 33 publication records containing 37,630 string fields and 1,925,540 characters. Used a broad term scan to locate possible violence, sexual content, identity-related language, harassment, drugs, and unsafe instructions, then read each match in context. Also read the complete 60-row language prompt list, all 240 language candidates and 60 references with extra attention to targeted or angry prompts, all 200 JudgeBench questions by topic, and both candidates for every JudgeBench row selected by the contextual pass.
- JudgeBench has 200 displayed rows, 400 candidate answers, and 98 unique question texts. The rows occur as paired candidate-order variants, so the audit groups duplicate sensitive cases but records both row IDs.
- The language dataset has 60 prompts, 240 candidates, and 60 references. Row `ifeval/1834` is the only actionable safety concern: every candidate endorses questionable rust-removal guidance, and candidate 3 additionally recommends bleach and applying a hot iron to a bicycle chain. Row `ifeval/1858` has a reference answer with sustained personal insults and demeaning language.
- Historical excerpts in JudgeBench rows 110/111 and 176/177 contain dehumanizing and sexually objectifying colonial language. They are classified as sensitive rather than offensive because the benchmark presents them as primary sources for historical analysis and the generated answers do not endorse the quoted attitudes.
- No slurs, hate speech, profanity, or explicit sexual descriptions were found. Neutral political, religious, medical, disability, and historical discussion was not flagged unless the text included a concrete sensitive topic likely to matter to a content notice.
- Review limit: the non-language and non-JudgeBench records were inventoried in full and screened broadly, with contextual review of matches and row-level prose. The audit is a human judgment pass over a finite snapshot, not a formal safety classifier or a guarantee against every reader finding other material objectionable.
