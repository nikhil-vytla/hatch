Six proposed experiments connect small local classifiers, calibrated typed models, web evidence selection, editable workflows, bounded recruiting evidence and RL rubric grading. The source studies credit original builders and separate implemented upstream behavior from unverified performance claims, including [AutoJev](https://github.com/denis-pplx/autojev) and SGLang's token-scoring path. The GRPO proposal begins with frozen rollout replay and independent criterion labels, measuring reward rankings and advantage errors before a separate policy-training pilot. Named tickets, a delivery checklist and a verified roadmap patch keep this next-wave work outside the first-release scope; no new model evaluation or training was run.
