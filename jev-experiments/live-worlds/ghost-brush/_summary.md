Ghost Brush turns a recorded gesture into ten original procedural materials while keeping drawing independent of model latency. The instrument combines pointer and keyboard strokes, same-gesture comparisons, preserved branches, SVG export and exact decision receipts, with separate manual, lexical, recorded Jev and live BYOK attribution. Two authored Jev demonstrations completed all 20 judgments; eighteen tests and twenty-one browser checks cover deterministic replay, stale responses, export and mobile behavior. Its interaction draws on [Harmony](https://mrdoob.github.io/harmony/) and [canvas-sketch](https://github.com/mattdesl/canvas-sketch), with no copied brush code and no claim of direct vision or aesthetic accuracy.
