Bramble Square is a continuous courtyard simulation where twelve fictional residents interpret an announcement, choose destinations and share cafés, books, music and garden space. Paired worlds support the same Jev controller with local fallback on or off, direct human intervention, replay and preserved branches; movement never waits for a model response. One genuine 24-answer Jev batch is replayable with exact input and raw-response provenance, while its 578 ms inference latency is clearly separated from instant playback. The original [Canvas](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Canvas_API) scene and [React](https://react.dev/reference/react/useEffect) controls passed sixteen engine tests plus desktop, mobile, dark-mode and delayed-response browser checks.
