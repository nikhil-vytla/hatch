# Independent evidence decides whether an experiment improved

Status: accepted, 2026-09-19.

Jev may select candidates, label training examples, or supply an optimization signal, but its own score cannot establish that the resulting system improved. Use held-out public labels, executable requirements, simulation outcomes, and optional blinded human judgments. Preserve failures, abstentions, budget exhaustion, and incomplete runs; never silently exclude them from a denominator.
