# Publish replayable experiments and preserve typed evidence

Status: accepted, 2026-09-19.

The user requested a Vercel app and adapters analogous to Pydantic in other languages. Deploy the gallery and compact research artifacts; keep live cloud calls authenticated and browser models local.
