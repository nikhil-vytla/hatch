03 / motion

Every action answers.

Rules and frequent controls respond immediately. The inspector appears in 120 ms from its own edge. Reduced motion removes transitions and starts the scene paused.

Sources and scope of this original study

This HTML, CSS and illustration were written for Jev. No third-party source was copied. It is a direction to review, not the released application or a model evaluation.

  • Wojciech Dobry's Gradients puts direct controls on the object and precise edits nearby.
  • Rauno Freiberg's interaction notes explain why frequent actions need immediate response and spatial consistency.
  • Emil Kowalski's animation examples inform restrained pressed feedback and source-related panel motion.
  • Josh W. Comeau's runnable figures put the example beside its explanation.
  • Paco Coursey's tabs give a selected state a stable place.
  • Maggie Appleton's Programming Portals motivates small editable rules within a visual tool.
