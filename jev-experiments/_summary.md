A [deployed Jev laboratory](https://jev-experiments.vercel.app) explores 25 visual experiments spanning procedural art, UI, music, routing, verification, context filtering, evaluation, and training. Public-dataset checks found that Jev's usefulness depends on the task: it lost to a trained linear baseline on Banking77, improved out-of-scope recognition on CLINC, and raised local Qwen instruction-following success from 21/40 to 23/40 through candidate selection. An original [Kev-inspired](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev) SmolLM2-360M decision model completed 240 training updates, improving four-way choice accuracy from 32.5% to 66.2% while leaving yes/no accuracy unchanged. The project includes distribution-preserving adapters for Python, TypeScript, Rust, and Go, recorded failures and costs, a small reusable checkpoint, and twenty further experiments with independent success criteria.
