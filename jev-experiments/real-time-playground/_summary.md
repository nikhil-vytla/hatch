A flight game and a crowd simulation explore how Jev can make asynchronous decisions while a world keeps moving. Visitors can vary artificial latency, compare short actions with lasting destinations, take control, and rewind into preserved branches. The default mode is explicitly a local timing simulation; actual Jev calls require a visitor-provided Gateway key. The interaction follows ideas from [Red Blob Games](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/a-star/introduction.html), with production decisions recorded as open questions rather than inferred from the sketch.
