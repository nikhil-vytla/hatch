A visual gallery searches 208 CC0 works from the [Cleveland Museum of Art](https://openaccess-api.clevelandart.org/), using the museum's metadata and curatorial descriptions to compare two Jev rankings with a deterministic keyword baseline. Six declared scene queries cover every artwork in both model modes, with 2,496/2,496 relevance scores recorded; these are exploratory judgments, not ground-truth search accuracy. The archive moved from the Art Institute of Chicago on 1 Oct 2026, because that museum's image server challenged visitors' browsers and about half the images failed to load. The model ranks text and does not inspect pixels.
