Jev's next-wave browser experiment will infer the next action from the current page and recent history, with a flight-search preparation flow as its first concrete example. The proposal credits [Composite's next-action research](https://research.composite.com/next-action) and uses [SelectiveNet](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/geifman19a.html) to frame learned abstention comparisons. It separates prediction coverage, complete action correctness and closed-loop task success, and preserves drafts through cancellation and bounded execution. The folder contains source notes, a decision ticket, delivery checklist and verified roadmap patch; browser implementation and model training remain open.
