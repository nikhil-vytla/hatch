The Mac toolkit installs an isolated [MLX](https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx) runtime, verifies pinned [Laya](https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya) files and a new 12 KB readout with SHA-256, and produces typed decisions or labels for explicitly supplied `.eml` files. The validation-selected experimental default passed a fresh installation with bundled credits, verified source bytes, and both authored first-action answers correct while networking was blocked. Eleven unit checks passed, and an installed readout matched 15 saved validation decisions within 1.05e-7 probability error. Both variants labeled only 7 of 12 authored emails correctly, so uncertainty and that limitation remain visible; inference dependencies stay separate from training and conversion tools.
