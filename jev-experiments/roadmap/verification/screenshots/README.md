# Visual verification captures

The six `*-final.png` files show the production home, materials, routing and local-study views after the craft and evidence-access changes. `visual-release.json` records their viewport, theme, reduced-motion setting, footer and overflow checks. The later publication correction changed only three unrendered training-status values; current public summaries now say training is complete.

The earlier `jev-release-*.png` files belong to the initial functional pass. They preserve crowd and music interaction states and the earlier home/material layouts; they are not current screenshots of every page. The separate [materials craft captures](../../playable/materials-craft/README.md) show painting/inspection, mobile dark mode and the offset touch preview. The [design reference board](../../design-research/reference-board.html) identifies external screenshots and their original builders.

Screenshots establish layout and visible state. Browser reports, actual downloads, source review and engine tests cover behavior separately. Neither these captures nor emulated mobile viewports establish physical-device accessibility or performance.

The final routing escalation and patch-parser corrections also postdate these captures. They change execution boundaries, with no visual change to the captured pages. The reports retain the original capture conditions.
