## Question and resolution

Version 1 uses choice, boolean and ordinal questions. Every response carries execution identity and timing. Successful distributions cover every declared option exactly once and sum to one. Unsupported, error and cancellation results contain explicit issues and no answers. Runtime limits are declared and enforced without truncation.
