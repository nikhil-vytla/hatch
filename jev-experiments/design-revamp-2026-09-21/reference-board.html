01 / Make the mechanism visible

The object carries the detail.

Bartosz CiechanowskiMechanical Watch

For Jev

Give the scene room. Put one useful control beside the consequence it produces, then explain the selected part in its own terms.

Keep the first action visible. A material's color, name and rule should stay connected as the reader moves between the scene and its note.

02 / Let the reader question it

A figure inside the argument.

Bret VictorExplorable Explanations

For Jev

Show one changed assumption beside the original case. Keep the relevant value and its consequences close enough to read together.

A routing preference can change a hypothetical choice. The measured result it refers to should remain fixed and visibly labeled.

03 / Preserve the reading thread

A little more, right here.

Nicky CaseNutshell

For Jev

Let "Why did this happen?" reveal a short explanation beside the selected object: its observation, the rule and the resulting change.

Preserve the reader's place and offer a clear way to close the explanation. Keep the central finding and essential controls in the open.

04 / Give the work a point of view

A reason to make the thing.

Matt WebbYesterday, on Interconnected

For Jev

Start the note with the question that led to the experiment. Give the figure room, then say what surprised us and what we would change next.

Make comparisons concrete: the original state, the changed state and a visible difference. Record an unfinished edge when it explains the next iteration.

Two more lenses / primary-page research, without captures

05 / Effect and implementation

Show the detail. Explain the choice.

Pair an adjustable example with the small part of the implementation that matters. Typography, hit areas and interrupted actions deserve the same care as the opening scene.

Jakub Krehel · interface details

06 / A useful counterexample

Let the limit be visible.

Explain what the algorithm sees, then show the case where the attractive first approach fails. Jev's experiment notes should connect that limit to a reproducible state and relevant code.
