Parallax compiles pinned public or private repositories into deterministic counterfactual coding tasks, with separate public starter and sealed evaluator capsules plus adapters for [HUD](https://hud.ai/) and [Prime Intellect Verifiers](https://github.com/PrimeIntellect-ai/verifiers). Twelve Click tasks rejected no-op, upstream restoration, and unrelated-path tampering, but live calibration showed that the family was novel rather than frontier-hard and exposed both a misaligned scope reward and a local containment failure. Further review found that counterfactual gold withholding can still leave an implementation-shaped shadow, so the experiment now distinguishes exact leakage, constrained completion, substrate familiarity, and clean synthesis. A typed variation pipeline models instruction, state, goals, constraints, verifier, budget, and provenance, then emits ten causal variant contracts with explicit intent, state, and verifier policies. A connected 25-note research graph records the evidence behind these decisions and treats variants from one source task as correlated interventions rather than independent benchmark samples.
