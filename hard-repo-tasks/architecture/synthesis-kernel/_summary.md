Parallax builds a pinned local GSM8K record and a hand-authored proposal fixture into one admitted family with static, matched, and evolved arms. The fixture is not Microsoft [Evolving Intent](https://github.com/microsoft/evolving-intent) output: it contains placeholder provenance strings and a known context/source mismatch, so the surviving claim is deterministic artifact lifecycle and locked replay only. `ConversationRun` executes caller-provided model callbacks, while workspace and checkpoint runtimes remain typed placeholders. The recorded suite and CLI proof established byte-identical artifacts for the manifested inputs, not generation provenance or semantic proposal validity.
