Parallax has a [project-local verification skill](SKILL.md) for its current CLI,
library, pytest, and HUD task-list paths. The family helper composes the direct
CLI commands into repeatable evidence capture, including an exact dirty-worktree
source manifest and retained failed-run diagnostics. Its hand-authored fixture
contains placeholder provenance strings and a known semantic mismatch, so the
proof establishes artifact determinism only. It supports Unit 0 of the
[research-protocol plan](../../../architecture/evolving-intent-pipeline/README.md)
without establishing Microsoft Evolving Intent generation, proposal integrity,
upstream parity, native asset feasibility, or a HUD rollout.
